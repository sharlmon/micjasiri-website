Who we are

Mic-Jasiri Productions.

Mic-Jasiri Productions is a registered limited liability production company based in Nairobi, Kenya, founded in 2024. We work from audiovisual strategy and concept development through filming, editing, graphic design and live stream production.

Our work spans video production, television commercials, event live streaming and conference coverage, with each production shaped around the client, the audience and the story.

Founder

Ali Sisei

Ali Sisei is the CEO and Founder of Mic-Jasiri Productions, bringing over 9 years of hands-on experience in film, television, and commercial production. He has worked on more than 50 productions spanning short films, reality TV, series, and corporate content.
